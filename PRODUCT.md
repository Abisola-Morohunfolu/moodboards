# Moodboard

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People quickly collecting ideas and plans on flexible visual boards. Rooms, weddings, projects, and everyday plans are examples, not required use cases. Planners and designers also organize client projects and share private board views.

## Product Purpose
Collect images, links, and notes on visual boards and organize them into sections. Make ideas easy to capture, revisit, and present. General board-making comes first; specialist features follow evidence from actual use.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current product supports password authentication, personal and business workspaces, blank boards, sections, notes, image uploads, link previews, desktop canvas movement, and mobile grids. Open boards support text/URL and image paste, image drop, multiple-image selection, and an in-memory progress tray with safe retries. Pending captures do not survive reloads. Personal workspaces are the default entry point. Signed-in users can search saved boards and items in a selected workspace, filter by board/type, navigate to an item, and restore deleted items from non-expiring board trash when they have editing permission. Business workspaces have clients and contacts with board-specific viewing links. The backend controls access, prices, link expiry, rotation, and revocation. Client-associated business boards support approver links, versioned approvals, rejection and swap requests, and planner feedback. Budgets, kits, billing, and public discovery are possible future features, not commitments or current marketing claims. The former planner-first launch gates and speculative pricing are superseded.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the Moodboard name and existing asterisk mark. The confirmed redesign is a quiet canvas informed by Figma's compact contextual tools and Pinterest's image emphasis. Use Instrument Sans, neutral surfaces, and a restrained blue accent. Support system light and dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing product documentation in docs/01-product.md and implemented routes and contracts. There are no verified testimonials or customer metrics to publish. Generated landing imagery and demonstration boards must be labeled as examples.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls and item movement, visible focus, labeled inputs, readable contrast, accessible dialogs, reduced-motion support, and responsive desktop/mobile layouts.
