# Moodboard

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People collecting inspiration for rooms, weddings, projects, and everyday plans. Planners and designers also organize client projects and share private board views.

## Product Purpose
Collect images, links, and notes on visual boards and organize them into sections. Make inspiration easy to revisit and present.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current product supports password authentication, personal and business workspaces, blank boards, sections, notes, image uploads, link previews, desktop canvas movement, and mobile grids. Business workspaces have clients and contacts with board-specific viewing links. The backend controls access, prices, link expiry, rotation, and revocation. Approvals, budgets, kits, billing, and public discovery are future features, not current marketing claims.

## Brand Commitments
Retain the Moodboard name and existing asterisk mark. The confirmed redesign is a quiet canvas informed by Figma's compact contextual tools and Pinterest's image emphasis. Use Instrument Sans, neutral surfaces, and a restrained blue accent. Support system light and dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing product documentation in docs/01-product.md and implemented routes and contracts. There are no verified testimonials or customer metrics to publish. Generated landing imagery and demonstration boards must be labeled as examples.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls and item movement, visible focus, labeled inputs, readable contrast, accessible dialogs, reduced-motion support, and responsive desktop/mobile layouts.
